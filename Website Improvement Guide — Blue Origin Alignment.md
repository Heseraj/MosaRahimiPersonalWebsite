### The Problem

This signals "student of AI" to a recruiter scanning in 5 seconds. Blue Origin needs someone who can **walk into the Office of the SVP-Operations and immediately add value** — not someone mid-pivot.

### Recommended Alternatives

**Option A (Operations-forward, aerospace signal):**

After two decades coordinating large-scale operations across public institutions, security forces, and advanced manufacturing, I work at the intersection of aerospace production systems, operational strategy, and the consequential problems complex organizations must solve at scale.

**Why this works for Blue Origin:** It positions you as an operations executive with aerospace manufacturing credibility — not an AI career-changer. The "consequential problems" framing signals strategic thinking without abandoning the technical dimension.
